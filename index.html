What Matters to Me

A few things I believe in.

Family

To me, being family-oriented means showing up for the people who matter, valuing those relationships and making time for them. I also believe marriage means creating a family in which one's partner becomes an equally important part of that circle.

People

I believe in standing up for people when I feel something is right — even when they are not part of my immediate team or there is no personal benefit involved. For me, fairness and ethics matter more when they are inconvenient than when they are easy.
